DAY 02 • ARUNOPS OBSERVABILITY

Monitoring vs Observability

Why traditional monitoring is not enough.

TOPIC 01

Monitoring

Monitoring watches predefined signals and alerts when known conditions or thresholds are violated.

TOPIC 02

Observability

Observability provides context for exploring system behavior and investigating unexpected issues.

TOPIC 03

Example

A CPU alert tells you CPU is high; correlated signals can help identify the workload or request causing it.

TOPIC 04

Key takeaway

Monitoring asks 'Is something wrong?' while observability helps answer 'Why is it wrong?'
