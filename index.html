01 — INTRODUCTION

What is Observability?

Observability is the ability to understand the internal state and behavior of a system by analyzing the data it produces.

Simple definition: Observability helps us answer: What is happening? Why is it happening? Where is it happening?
02 — WHY OBSERVABILITY?

Why Monitoring is Not Enough?

Monitoring tells us that something is wrong; observability provides the context needed to investigate why.

Monitoring

Watches predefined conditions and alerts when known thresholds or failure conditions are reached.

Observability

Uses rich telemetry and correlation to investigate known and unexpected system behavior.

03 — CORE CONCEPT

Observability Pillars

The traditional pillars are Metrics, Logs and Traces.

📊 Metrics

Numerical measurements that tell us what is happening over time.

📜 Logs

Timestamped records that provide detailed information about system events.

🔭 Traces

End-to-end records showing how a request travels across services.
