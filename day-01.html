DAY 01 • ARUNOPS OBSERVABILITY

Observability Fundamentals

Introduction, observability, monitoring and telemetry.

TOPIC 01

What is Observability?

Observability is the ability to understand a system's internal state from the data it produces.

TOPIC 02

Why do we need it?

Modern distributed systems have many services and dependencies, making failures harder to investigate.

TOPIC 03

Telemetry

Telemetry is the data emitted by systems that gives engineers visibility into behavior and health.

TOPIC 04

SRE connection

Observability signals help SREs detect, investigate, resolve and verify production issues.
